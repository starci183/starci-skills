## Record

Grammar is a versioned, business-free package of generic leaves, branches, composites, invariants, variable axes, neutral treatments, and consequential complex cases.

## Package boundary

An application directly installs `@starci/ui/common` and exactly one routed `@starci/grammar/<id>`. Generic exports keep their layer, for example `@starci/grammar/core/branch/SurfaceCard` or `@starci/grammar/core/composites/Sidebar`. The lock records exact version, integrity, export path/name, layer, contract hash, template hash, and declared dependencies. No semver range, alternate barrel, anatomy-changing wrapper, or implicit grammar inheritance is accepted.

Grammar contains no actor, product entity, business operation, price, entitlement, policy, domain state, or business outcome. An opaque Product Block maps domain state to neutral presentation state; Grammar maps the neutral state to generic treatment.

## Complex-case reasoning

Complex cases define how exact generic owners compose under consequential render-neutral conditions: nested surface depth, zero/one/many rows, long content, mixed neutral states, interactive versus static rows, dividers, focus, overflow, and compact reflow.

A labelled peer list inside an outer surface may require a distinct nested `SurfaceListCard` owner. The outer `SurfaceCard` owns the page-level surface and external label; the nested branch owns row boundaries and repeated state treatment. A row in neutral state `affirmative` may render the grammar's exact check treatment. Grammar knows only collection structure and neutral state, so the result feels bounded and trustworthy without inventing business legitimacy.

Reject surface-in-surface used only for decoration, ambiguous padding ownership, nested owners with the same responsibility, local recreation of a missing lower tier, and business-bearing example data. `global.css` may change declared color-token values only.
