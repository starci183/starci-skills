## Record

Backend planning joins accepted behavior with live schema and complete sibling-family evidence. Qdrant locates candidates; exact checkout files prove field names, owners, layering, exceptions, tests, and conventions. Every reached pattern situation binds to exact paths and evidence.

The boundary plan names every create/modify/delete path, responsibility, dependency, branch, test, gate, exclusion, and proof before source writes. Challenge reopens all evidence. Implementation begins only from an approval bound to the canonical plan hash and may mutate only approved paths. Missing precedent is declared as a new family; boundary drift returns to planning.
