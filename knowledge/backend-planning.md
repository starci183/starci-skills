## Record

Backend planning joins accepted behavior with one fresh business receipt and one hash-pinned coding-context generation. Qdrant locates candidates; the canonical generated JSON records and content hashes prove field names, owners, layering, exceptions, tests, and conventions. Every reached pattern situation binds to exact contract records and evidence. Raw checkout source is not loaded while planning.

The boundary plan names every create/modify/delete path, responsibility, dependency, branch, test, gate, exclusion, and proof before source writes. Challenge reopens all evidence. Implementation begins only from an approval bound to the canonical plan hash, a current source commit, and exact target hashes; only then may it open and mutate approved checkout files. Missing precedent is declared as a new family; source or boundary drift invalidates the approval and returns to planning.
