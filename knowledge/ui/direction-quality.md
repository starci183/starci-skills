# Direction quality before proof

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Knowledge ID | `fe.ui.direction-quality` |
| Operators | `fe/request-compile, fe/direction-generate, fe/visual-fidelity` |
| Search tags | `dominant direction, alternatives, product family, benchmark, material difference` |
| Dependencies | approved user outcome; `fe.ui.hierarchy`; `fe.ui.ownership-composition`; routed Grammar Core and product DNA |

Direction quality is compiled into the ordinary v7.6 path, not exposed as another public stage. A
`refine` change skips generation, as does a `new` or `reconstruct` change whose compile evidence binds
an exact approved direction by its typed identity, content fingerprint, and direction-specific
approval authority. Generic approval labels do not establish that binding. Otherwise `new` or `reconstruct` uses one `fe/direction-generate`:
`dominant` emits one inspectable direction and continues without waiting;
`alternatives` emits exactly three or four materially different directions plus one real local HTML
comparison and waits for the exact selected id.

Every rendered direction preserves the compiled acceptance, selected Grammar decision manifest,
iconography and media provenance, and product-family benchmark identities. Producer narration, an
inline-only screenshot, a recovery/error state standing in for the populated happy case, or aesthetic
variation without a material ownership/composition difference is not direction evidence.

Product-family benchmarks calibrate native quality; they never become authority for page facts or a
license to copy a screenshot. Direction generation cannot close visual quality. Source apply, complete
capture/preflight, adversarial probes, and the fresh blind `fe/visual-fidelity` verdict remain mandatory.
