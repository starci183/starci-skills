SurfaceCard owns one block responsibility and applies contour, radius, background, and offset depth as one package-defined system.

- Allow at most the declared focal accent: colored header field, corner marker, controlled rotation, or offset variant.
- Keep body alignment, labels, facts, and actions stable despite expressive framing.
- Across peer cards, align content anchors and action regions before varying accent.
- Nominate a focal card or controlled pattern; do not randomize rotation.
- Sticky cards normally remove rotation and reduce offset depth.
- Loading and error states avoid animating every decorative layer.

Verify short/long content, peers, actionless, many actions, loading, empty, error, sticky, focus clipping, and narrow screens. Reject unrelated decorative objects, random shadow directions, and local anatomy overrides.
