Offset Pop is the expressive Grammar built from strong contours, offset depth, decisive color fields, editorial typography, and controlled asymmetry. Personality comes from a limited number of deliberate accents, not maximum decoration on every object.

Global laws: one contour system, one offset-depth direction, role-bound colors, few rotated/overlapping accents, stable operational geometry, visible focus, and responsive simplification before content loss.
