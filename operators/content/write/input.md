# Input

Provide the frozen teacher brief, language/style authority, bounded article destinations, audience, and any exact review findings.

## Contract fields

- `context.briefRefs`: Exact frozen teacher brief and revision references.
- `context.styleRefs`: Exact language, terminology, and editorial authority references.
- `input.articleTargetRefs`: Bounded article destination references for requested editions.
- `input.naturalLanguages`: Natural languages that must be written from the same brief.
- `input.audience`: Learner audience whose reading level controls the prose.
- `input.revisionFindingRefs`: Exact review findings to fix without broadening the brief.
