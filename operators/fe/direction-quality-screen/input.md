# `fe/direction-quality-screen` input

Bind one latest-source representative raster, the selected direction, exact product-family benchmark
rasters, one fresh `gpt-5.6-sol` execution, and the fixed `9/10` continuation threshold. The screen is
an inexpensive pre-proof gate; it is not the final blind matrix review and consumes no visual repair
round.

`attempt` counts fresh direction attempts. Attempts one and two may return `rebrainstorm`. A third
sub-9 result must request the smallest Business or Backend supplementation needed by the approved
user outcome instead of rendering the same premise again.

## Contract fields

- `context.authorityRefs`: Exact approved user-outcome and Business authority preserved by this screen.
- `context.evidenceRefs`: Content-addressed raster and benchmark evidence available to this job.
- `context.uiKnowledgeId`: Mandatory UI-law root used only after the pixel-first observation.
- `context.sourceFingerprint`: Latest product-source fingerprint represented by the raster.
- `context.reviewerModel`: Exact fresh visual reviewer model, fixed to `gpt-5.6-sol`.
- `context.reviewerCount`: Exactly one reviewer owns this atomic screen.
- `context.contextIsolation`: Fresh-context isolation that prevents producer self-review.
- `context.forkTurns`: No inherited turns may bias the first-glance judgement.
- `context.reviewerExecutionRef`: Content-addressed receipt for the reviewer execution.
- `context.debug`: Required observable CALL, RETURN, dimension scores, and transition trace.
- `input.targetRef`: Exact browser-visible surface being screened.
- `input.directionRef`: Current direction invalidated when its score is below nine.
- `input.representativeRasterRef`: One fresh latest-source raster that represents the direction.
- `input.benchmarkRasterRefs`: Approved product-family rasters used to judge native quality.
- `input.attempt`: Fresh direction-attempt number, from one through three.
- `input.minimumScore`: Fixed production continuation threshold of nine.
