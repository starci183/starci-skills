# `fe/direction-quality-screen` output

Return one score from the arithmetic mean, rounded down, of exactly six visible dimensions. Copy the
latest source fingerprint, acceptance, requested/observed state, durable raster artifact, benchmark
rasters, and fresh reviewer execution receipt into the result so invocation validation can reject
inline-only, stale, self-scored, or wrong-state evidence:
composition, hierarchy, density/empty-space balance, product-family DNA, content/media purpose, and
action clarity.

- `continue`: score 9 or 10; the direction may enter deep capture/proof.
- `rebrainstorm`: score below 9 on attempt one or two; invalidate the current direction and render a
  fresh direction without spending a visual repair round.
- `business-required`: approved Business needs the smallest additive or purpose-aligning supplement;
  never replace the approved outcome merely to make the page attractive.
- `backend-required`: approved data capability is insufficient for the supplemented Business.
- `blocked`: required raster, benchmark, or execution evidence is missing.

## Contract fields

- `output.outcome`: Typed continuation, rebrainstorm, smallest-owner handoff, or blocked result.
- `output.result`: Six dimension scores, floored overall score, attempt, owner, and raster identity.
- `output.gaps`: Exact visible or authority gaps that prevent a nine-point direction.
- `output.evidenceRefs`: Exact content-addressed evidence inspected by the fresh reviewer.
- `output.handoff`: Correlated Business or Backend supplement mission; null for local continuation.
