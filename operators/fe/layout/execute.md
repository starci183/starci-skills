into responsive layout geometry. The result must bind the same direction, law gate, and semantic details and freeze distinct wide, intermediate, and
compact states. Layout cannot issue or replace a UI-law binding and cannot apply Grammar Core.

Use only the closed `context` and `input`. Return one typed `output.outcome` plus this job's result, gaps, and evidence. Do not route, persist a session, or choose another operator.

Frontend creation follows `AI-first -> Rules-first -> Grammar-last`. Direction synthesis, mandatory
law validation, semantic-detail freeze, layout compilation, Grammar Core compilation, implementation, and audit are separate jobs.
