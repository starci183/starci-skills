# service.operate — brief

Read operator.md at your step; write only response/ of your branch; replace the running response.json skeleton before you exit; * marks a fallback stop.

## Job

Bring one auxiliary service the environment declares — an observability stack, a code-quality service, a tunnel beside a served runtime — to the state that declaration asks for, prove from its own probe that it is in that state, and record the holder that answers, under the lease that orders sessions.

## Done when

Done when the `service-receipt` binds the one service the environment declares and the authority that covered it, and its Checks prove the observed state from the service's own probe — an `up` that answers, a `down` whose port is free — with the holder that answers recorded and no credential value anywhere in it.

Primary output: `service-receipt`

## Inputs

none

## Outputs

`service-receipt` `response/response.md`

## Stops

`INVALID_INPUT`, `NO_PROGRESS`, `SERVICE_APPROVAL_REQUIRED`, `SERVICE_UNPROVEN`
