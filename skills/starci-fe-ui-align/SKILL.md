## NESTED SKILLS

None. This capability owns cross-surface audit, authority correction, source convergence and proof in one invocation.

## Purpose

Challenge whether equivalent UI responsibilities resolve to the same semantic owner and visual expression, and whether distinct responsibilities remain visibly distinct, across a declared existing surface set. Converge on current evidence-backed authority rather than majority style. Strengthen durable authority only when a real counterexample proves that the current grammar, principle, pattern or gate cannot express the required invariant.

## Scope boundary

The caller declares one routed frontend and a closed surface set containing at least two independent occurrences of a semantic responsibility. The occurrences may span routes, states or repeated regions on one route. Audit their complete real-page parents, not isolated screenshots or component demos. New composition, one-block anatomy work, one concrete owner-feedback correction and one already-understood local defect remain outside this cross-surface capability.

## Process

1. **Audit and challenge — read only.** Resolve `defaultLang`, Source, project, routed FE, grammar/profile, business heads and committed baselines. Print the shared two-row step table. Inventory the declared routes, reachable states, reference viewports, semantic responsibilities, contract keys and exact `ComponentBase → Component → PageBase/LayoutBase/OverlayBase → Page/Layout/Overlay` owner chains. Capture the complete real pages and build the four-lock baseline. Cluster equivalent responsibilities separately from intentionally distinct responsibilities. For every variance, trace symptom → expected outcome → evidence → governing authority → counterexample → classification → impact cone, then state whether the owner's observation is `correct`, `incorrect` or `partly-correct`, why, why the AI has not executed it, and the exact authority and source consequence. Run grammar, principle and dependency checks before proposing a change. Produce one canonical convergence proposal, every justified deviation, every affected authority/source/test path and the same-state/same-viewport proof plan under one exact `### NEED APPROVALS` boundary.
2. **Align and prove — after `OK`.** Record the approved revision and target baseline before the first write. When authority changes, update paired English/Vietnamese records first, compile runtime context, validate all dependency graphs and add or update executable enforcement where the invariant is machine-observable. Correct every affected consumer inside the approved cone without changing unrelated composition or product behavior. Run canonical gates and full-page browser comparison for each affected responsibility at the approved states and viewports. Repair in-boundary defects until the declared surface set is consistent and zero known defects remain. Reconcile business authority only when product truth changed.

## Classification

Every observed variance receives exactly one verdict:

- `justified-deviation`: a declared role, profile, state, viewport failure or page-level exception owns the difference;
- `source-drift`: authority is sound and one or more consumers diverged;
- `inventory-or-application-miss`: the correct owner or existing rule was not reached;
- `grammar-gap` or `grammar-misruling`: a stable product-family fact, outcome, owner or durable behavior is absent or contradicted;
- `principle-gap` or `principle-misruling`: a product-neutral visual situation is absent or contradicted in the smallest real module;
- `pattern-or-gate-gap`: source ownership or an observable invariant lacks accountable enforcement.

Frequency, age, visual similarity and local preference are evidence, never authority. `misruling` requires a concrete counterexample. A new grammar or principle situation requires an unexpressible case, a positive scope, a negative boundary and an impact search across all routed consumers.

## Rules

1. Semantic equivalence is proved before visual equality; pixel similarity cannot merge different responsibilities.
2. The canonical expression follows business truth, routed grammar/profile, MASTER, contracts and governed source; majority style never wins by count.
3. Read the complete real-page parent and exact owner chain for every occurrence before classifying drift.
4. If existing authority already resolves the case, change source rather than law.
5. Grammar owns product-family facts, outcomes, owners and durable behavior; principles own only product-neutral visual situations left unresolved by higher authority.
6. A shared authority change expands the impact cone to every routed consumer. If that cone exceeds the displayed boundary, return to approval before writing.
7. Preserve justified deviations explicitly; alignment never means flattening roles, states or responsive behavior.
8. Authority changes precede source changes and carry paired publications, compiled runtime context and executable proof where observable.
9. Audit evidence and comparison previews remain in project cache; source, tests, authority records and browser proof are the durable outcome.
10. The declared surface set converges in one invocation; no consumer is silently left on a temporary mixed system.
11. Before approval, compile one authority-to-write map: one decision has one semantic owner, every touched file belongs to one write batch, and preserved owners are explicitly excluded.
12. A repeated defect strengthens the smallest missing durable layer. Sound law is not duplicated: add a case, pattern, gate or source assertion when enforcement or application failed.
13. `Why not executed` names the real cause: approval boundary, missing evidence, external blocker or AI reasoning/reporting failure. `Waiting for OK` alone is not an explanation.

## Stops

- Missing or stale frontend route, grammar/profile, business truth, committed baseline or closed surface set.
- Fewer than two independent occurrences of the responsibility under review.
- The target experience or page composition does not yet exist — larger owner: `starci-fe-design-layout`.
- The request changes only one block anatomy — larger owner: `starci-fe-design-block`.
- The trigger is one concrete owner-feedback correction — larger owner: `starci-fe-feedback-evolve`.
- The request is one already-understood local defect — larger owner: `starci-fe-minor-fix`.
- An occurrence cannot be reproduced in its complete real-page parent.
- A proposed canonical expression has no authority-backed reason and needs an undisclosed product decision.
- A grammar/principle change is already expressible by current law or lacks its required counterexample and negative boundary.
- A shared authority change reaches consumers outside the approved impact cone.
- Business-affecting work lacks an `in-progress` feature head.
- A required gate or visual defect cannot be repaired inside the approved boundary.

## OUTPUT

Render a compact owner-feedback verdict table with columns `Observation`, `Verdict`, `Why`, `Why not executed`, `Authority correction`, `Source correction` and `Proof`. Then report the audited surface set, equivalence/distinction clusters, justified deviations, canonical recommendation, one-pass authority-to-write map and exact source/proof boundary. On completion, update the same table with strengthened authority, aligned consumers, preserved owners, business status, gates and same-state/same-viewport full-page proof.
