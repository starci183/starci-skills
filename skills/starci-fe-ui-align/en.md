## NESTED SKILLS

None.

## Purpose

Challenge whether equivalent UI responsibilities resolve to the same semantic owner and visual expression, and whether distinct responsibilities remain visibly distinct, across a declared existing surface set. Converge on current evidence-backed authority rather than majority style. Strengthen durable authority only when a real counterexample proves that current law cannot express the required invariant.

## Scope boundary

The scope is one routed frontend and a closed set with at least two independent occurrences of one semantic responsibility. Audit complete real-page parents. New composition, one-block anatomy work, one concrete owner-feedback correction and one known local defect remain outside this capability.

## Process

1. Audit read-only: resolve routes, authority and baselines; inventory complete pages, states, viewports, semantic roles, contract keys and exact owner chains; separate equivalent from intentionally distinct responsibilities; classify every variance; and state whether each owner observation is `correct`, `incorrect` or `partly-correct`, why, why the AI has not executed it, and the exact authority/source consequence. Produce one canonical proposal plus exact authority/source/proof boundary.
2. After `OK`, take baselines, update proven authority gaps before source, rebuild runtime context and enforcement, align every approved consumer, then run gates and same-state/same-viewport full-page proof until zero known defects remain.

## Classification

Use exactly one verdict per variance: `justified-deviation`, `source-drift`, `inventory-or-application-miss`, `grammar-gap`, `grammar-misruling`, `principle-gap`, `principle-misruling`, or `pattern-or-gate-gap`. Frequency is not authority; misruling requires a counterexample, and new law requires positive scope, negative boundary and a complete routed impact search.

## Rules

1. Prove semantic equivalence before visual equality.
2. Majority style never selects the canonical expression.
3. Existing authority means source changes, not new law.
4. Grammar owns product-family truth; principles own unresolved product-neutral visual situations.
5. Shared law expands the impact cone to every routed consumer.
6. Preserve justified deviations and complete the declared convergence in one invocation.
7. Authority changes precede source and carry paired publication, compiled context and executable proof.
8. Cache is temporary; authority, source, tests and browser proof are durable.
9. Before approval, compile one authority-to-write map: one decision has one semantic owner, every touched file belongs to one write batch, and preserved owners are explicitly excluded.
10. A repeated defect strengthens the smallest missing durable layer. Sound law is not duplicated: add a case, pattern, gate or source assertion when enforcement or application failed.
11. `Why not executed` names the real cause: approval boundary, missing evidence, external blocker or AI reasoning/reporting failure. `Waiting for OK` alone is not an explanation.

## Stops

- Missing route, authority, baseline, reproducible real-page parent or closed multi-occurrence scope.
- New page composition — larger owner: `starci-fe-design-layout`.
- One block anatomy — larger owner: `starci-fe-design-block`.
- One concrete owner-feedback correction — larger owner: `starci-fe-feedback-evolve`.
- One already-understood local defect — larger owner: `starci-fe-minor-fix`.
- An ungrounded canonical choice, an already-expressible law change, or a missing counterexample/negative boundary.
- Impact outside the approved cone, missing business authority, or an in-boundary defect that cannot be repaired.

## OUTPUT

Render an owner-feedback verdict table with `Observation`, `Verdict`, `Why`, `Why not executed`, `Authority correction`, `Source correction` and `Proof`. Report the audited set, justified deviations, canonical recommendation, one-pass authority-to-write map and exact approval boundary; then update the same table with authority changes, aligned consumers, preserved owners, gates and full-page visual proof.
