## IMPACT ROUTING

Use this skill only for a consistency question spanning a closed set of existing surfaces with at least two
independently implemented consumers, or an explicit systemic authority audit backed by a concrete counterexample
and complete consumer inventory. An exact local mismatch stays a plain `micro` correction or returns to the
Layout Refactor owner. New page/flow anatomy returns to the Layout owner; missing product truth
returns to business authority. Do not promote a preference into shared law to justify this path.

## Run

Read `@skill-shape` and `@orchestration` first and resolve `defaultLang`. The caller supplies the project, FE role,
closed surface set and concrete consistency question. Initial discovery is read-only. Follow the shared step table
and approval protocol; only the displayed `approve-apply` boundary may authorize authority or product source writes.

### 1. Bind the comparison

Verify workspace routing and the current business head. Freeze visible facts, actions, permissions, routes, states,
data ownership and backend contracts. Name each surface, semantic region, source owner, representative state and
desktop/narrow viewport. Record pre-existing dirt and stop on unattributable overlap. A screenshot may identify a
question but never defines the expected system by itself.

### 2. Resolve declared authority independently

Run grammar resolution for every compared surface. Read only active profile/fact/rule/capsule/case/template
authority, then the selected principles, FE patterns and gates. Record what must be shared, what may intentionally
differ and which owner decides each outcome. Grammar owns product-family meaning and behavior. Principles own only
product-neutral visual situations that remain after the direct semantic owner and child set are known.

### 3. Measure the real product independently

Trace the live owner chain and render matched reachable states and viewports. Inventory hierarchy, component
ownership, density, tokens, responsive composition, focus order, accessibility semantics and interactions. Use
the integrated quality lenses and detector families as challenge evidence, not as replacement authority. Do not
show this track a proposed grammar or principle change before its observation receipt passes.

### 4. Reconcile and classify

For every discrepancy issue one verdict: `intentional-variation`, `local-application-drift`, `grammar-gap`,
`grammar-misruling`, `principle-gap`, `principle-misruling`, `pattern-or-gate-gap` or `product-truth-conflict`.
Frequency alone never makes behavior correct. Evolve grammar or principles only when a binding invariant is
violated, at least two independent cases support the same generalization, or the owner explicitly rules it
systemic. A sound authority with a bad consumer remains an application/enforcement failure.

Build the exact impact cone from each changed authority owner through compiled context, patterns/gates and every
routed FE consumer. Product-truth conflicts stop and return to business authority. New anatomy stops and returns
to Layout. Local-only findings are removed from the systemic write batch and handed to the proportional path.

### 5. Approve and apply one boundary

Display one exact boundary containing only required paired English/Vietnamese authority, executable positive and
counterexample cases, compiler/gate updates, affected FE consumers and tests. Exact `OK` authorizes it once. Take
baselines after approval and before writing. Update the highest failed authority first, compile runtime context,
then validate grammar, principles and dependency graphs before changing a dependent consumer. Preserve sound law;
do not copy it into a second owner. Apply the resolved outcome to every consumer in the approved impact cone.

### 6. Prove stable consistency

Run canonical FE gates and authority validators. Capture the real connected product at the same states and
viewports used by the observation track, including keyboard and responsive behavior. The final proof binds the
stable authority hashes, consumer heads, test results and computed visual/accessibility/interaction evidence.

## Rules

1. This skill reconciles existing cross-surface UI; it does not invent a journey, capability or page anatomy.
2. Declared authority and observed product evidence remain independent until both gates pass.
3. A majority implementation is evidence, never authority by vote.
4. Intentional variation is preserved when product truth or an exact semantic owner requires it.
5. Grammar changes require product-family evidence; principle changes require product-neutral evidence.
6. Every durable authority change includes paired publications, compiled context and executable regression cases.
7. Authority changes precede dependent FE changes and cover every approved consumer in the impact cone.
8. One local preference, screenshot or outlier cannot create shared law.
9. Product truth, backend source, seed data and provider state are never writable here.
10. Completion requires green authority, dependency, FE and real-product consistency proof.

## Stops

- Fewer than two independent consumers and no explicit systemic authority audit with a concrete counterexample.
- Missing or stale business truth, an unrouteable surface or an unreproducible comparison claim.
- The requested result requires new product behavior, page/flow anatomy, backend work or seed changes.
- A claimed systemic rule has no independent cases or would merely encode one implementation preference.
- The impact cone, exact authority owner or full consumer set cannot be closed before approval.
- A red authority, dependency, FE or product proof cannot be repaired inside the approved boundary.

## Output

Report the closed surface set, consistency verdicts, intentional variations, highest failed owners, approved
authority/source boundary, grammar or principle changes (if any), aligned consumers and final computed proof.
State explicitly when authority was sound and only product application or enforcement changed.
