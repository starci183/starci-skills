## IMPACT ROUTING

Use this skill for a closed set of existing surfaces with at least two independently implemented consumers, or
an explicit systemic authority audit backed by a concrete counterexample and complete consumer inventory. Exact
local corrections use the plain path or Layout Refactor. New anatomy returns to Layout; product-truth changes
return to business authority.

## Run

### 1. Bind the comparison

Read `@skill-shape` and `@orchestration`, resolve `defaultLang`, then bind the project, FE role, closed surface set
and concrete consistency question. Freeze business truth, states, viewports, source heads and writable roots.

### 2. Resolve declared authority independently

Resolve grammar and principle owners without adapting them to current source. Record required shared outcomes,
intentional variation and the exact durable owner of each expectation.

### 3. Measure the real product independently

Measure live source and real matched states/viewports without showing this track a proposed law change. Record
source, visual, responsive, accessibility and interaction evidence.

### 4. Reconcile and classify

Keep declared authority and observed product evidence independent until both pass, then classify every discrepancy
as intentional variation, local application drift, grammar gap/misruling, principle gap/misruling,
pattern-or-gate gap or product-truth conflict. Build the full consumer impact cone.

### 5. Approve and apply one boundary

Evolve durable authority only when a binding invariant is violated, at least two independent cases support the
same generalization, or the owner explicitly rules it systemic. Under exact `OK`,
update the highest failed authority first, add executable positive and counterexample cases, compile runtime
context and pass grammar/principle/dependency gates before aligning every approved FE consumer.

### 6. Prove stable consistency

Prove the same real states and viewports with FE gates and computed visual, accessibility and interaction evidence.

## Rules

1. Reconcile existing UI; never invent journeys, capabilities or page anatomy.
2. Majority implementation is evidence, not authority by vote.
3. Preserve intentional variation required by product truth or semantic ownership.
4. Grammar changes require product-family evidence; principles require product-neutral evidence.
5. One local preference, screenshot or outlier cannot create shared law.
6. Paired publications, compiled context and executable cases accompany every authority change.
7. Authority changes precede dependent FE changes and cover the approved impact cone.
8. Product truth, backend source, seeds and provider state remain fixed.
9. Completion requires green authority, dependency, FE and real-product proof.

## Stops

- The comparison is local-only, unrouteable or unreproducible.
- Product truth is stale or the result requires new behavior, anatomy, backend work or seed changes.
- A systemic claim lacks independent cases, an exact owner or a closed consumer impact cone.
- A red gate cannot be repaired inside the approved boundary.

## Output

Report the surface set, verdicts, intentional variations, highest failed owners, approved boundary, authority
changes (if any), aligned consumers and computed proof. Say explicitly when authority was sound and only product
application or enforcement changed.
