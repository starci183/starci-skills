# Output

Completion, choice wait, blocker, or typed cross-domain handoff for the same mission.

Every terminal binds runtime/source fingerprints and one structured evidence packet. `complete`
requires the live-ready direction screen, durable representative raster, blind visual result,
quality, UAT, tests, and build receipts. `fixture-passed` may only be a typed handoff and still requires
the direction, durable raster, blind review, tests, and build evidence. A pre-proof handoff, choice
wait, or block names its stage explicitly. Prose, inline screenshots, reconstructed scores, and an
invisible terminal do not satisfy this output.
