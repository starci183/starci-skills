# starci-content-generate

Generate or refactor one evidence-backed educational content unit. Own one durable boundary: the reviewed lesson bundle and its executable proof.

Follow `machine.json` exactly. `content/brainstorm` uses one fresh Sol execution as the teacher: it defines learner prerequisites, observable learning outputs, examples, misconceptions, scope, and add/change/remove dispositions. Luna realizes the frozen brief through writing, no more than four implementation-language tracks, and the E2E repair loop. `content/image` renders only approved brief material. `content/review` uses one fresh Sol execution after the full bundle exists and routes precise revisions back to their owning stage.

Never treat generated prose, a visual, or AI confidence as proof. E2E completion requires commands, exit codes, and inspectable evidence. A revision cannot weaken the frozen learning contract or tests merely to close the mission.
