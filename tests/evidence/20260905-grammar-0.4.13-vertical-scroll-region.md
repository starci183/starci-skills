## The defect, measured

The Nivo Setup audit at 0.4.12 (session `20260905-170300-nivo-environment.preflight`,
`step-5/parallel-2/response/data/verdicts.json`) measured the node `region "Tin nhắn thiết lập"` — the
conversation `VerticalScrollRegion` of `ChatWorkspace` — on four captures (`module-setup-vi-wide`,
`module-setup-vi-narrow`, `module-setup-empty-vi-narrow`, `module-setup-read-only-vi-wide`): stamped
`MEASURE-7 OVERFLOW-3`, rendering `overflow: auto auto`, verdict fail, routed to `grammar-gap`. The
same node had measured the same way at 0.4.11. `MEASURE-7` on the same node passed on every capture
(the region scrolls inside the 20rem cap the consumer chose); only the axis claim failed.

The cause is the one 0.4.11 repaired on the horizontal sibling, on the region nobody had measured
yet. The vendor's `.scroll-shadow--vertical` (`@heroui/styles` 3.2.4) sets `overflow-y: auto` alone,
and the family's own `.starci-core-chat-workspace-conversation` does the same; nothing in the family's
sheet named `overflow-x` for a vertical region. Per CSS a computed non-visible overflow on one axis
forces the other from `visible` to `auto`, so a sheet that names one axis renders two.

## The repair (package commit 41be7227)

Six files inside `packages/grammar`, the plan's exact file set:

- `src/core/composite/VerticalScrollRegion/index.tsx` takes `overflow?: "always" | "needed"` (default
  `always`), the shape `HorizontalScrollRegion` has had since 0.4.11, stamps
  `data-grammar-scroll-region="vertical"` and `data-grammar-overflow`, and claims `MEASURE-7 OVERFLOW-3`
  or `MEASURE-7 OVERFLOW-4` accordingly. `{...regionProps}` is still spread first, so a host's
  passed-in `data-contract` is replaced by the region's own: one claim per node. No className, style,
  tag or JSX text changed, which the operator's projection gate would have refused.
- `src/common/styles.css` declares `overflow-x: hidden; overflow-y: auto` on
  `[data-grammar-scroll-region="vertical"]`, beside the horizontal rule. Ruling: no `!important`, the
  opposite of 0.4.12's Heading ruling, because no host class (`chat-workspace-conversation`,
  `chat-workspace-overlay-rail`, `accordion-scroll-region`, `form-scroll-viewport`) and no vendor rule
  ever names `overflow-x`, so the declaration is unopposed in either consumer import order. A later
  host that names `overflow-x` is the case that revisits this rule.
- `ChatWorkspace/index.tsx` is untouched on purpose: it asks for no `overflow`, so its conversation
  and rails keep claiming `OVERFLOW-3`, which is the identifier the consumer's before audit failed
  and its after audit must pass by the same name (`operators/library-update/validate.mjs#auditProofErrors`
  matches claims by identifier). Asking for `needed` there would have moved the claim to `OVERFLOW-4`
  and broken the consumer's two-halves proof.
- `package.json` 0.4.12 → 0.4.13; `CHANGELOG.md` gains the 0.4.13 section above 0.4.12.

## The tests

Two paired cases, both red at the base and green at the commit (`before` exit 1: 2 failed / 35 passed
of 37 across the four regression specs, the Tabs and Heading cases green at base; `after` exit 0):

- `src/core/scrollable-surfaces.spec.tsx`, `gives VerticalScrollRegion one overflow answer and one
  scrolling axis`: the mirror of the 0.4.11 HorizontalScrollRegion case — the stamp read off static
  markup, the two declarations read out of the shipped sheet for the region's identity selector, and
  `overflow="needed"` moving stamp and claim together to `OVERFLOW-4`.
- `src/core/composition/ChatWorkspace/index.spec.tsx` (jsdom), `renders the conversation region on the
  one axis its OVERFLOW-3 stamp claims, measured per axis` — the plan's regression assertion: renders
  the workspace under the shipped sheet (lifted out of its `@layer`, read through
  `fileURLToPath`), takes the region by its role and the audited label, and reads
  `getComputedStyle` per axis. At the base the inline axis measures `visible` (the failing message:
  `the conversation must stay clipped on the inline axis: expected 'visible' to be 'hidden'`); after
  the repair `auto` / `hidden`.

The regression command carries the 0.4.11 and 0.4.12 specs (`scrollable-surfaces`, `Tabs`,
`Heading`) beside the new cases, so the frameless SurfaceCard, the HorizontalScrollRegion under Tabs
and the Heading FONT scale are proved green on both sides of the change.

One measurement fact the next spec author needs, recorded in the receipt and the changelog: jsdom's
`getComputedStyle` does not expand the `overflow` shorthand into per-axis values and does not model
the axis coupling, so it reports the declared cascade rather than a browser's used values. That is
why the ChatWorkspace mock's `data-grammar-scroll="contained"` (which carries `overflow: hidden` in
the sheet) did not make the base case pass, and why the browser audit remains the witness to what the
surface does.
