3. FIXED ANATOMY (the part generators get wrong - containment is literal)

     the beak does not merge with the head outline.
   - One small dark eye on the head's upper-right quadrant; one subtle
     darker wing oval centered on the body. No legs, no feet, no water,
     no props, no second duck.
