Data ethics foundations

Build a practical framework for recognizing risk, explaining trade-offs, and making responsible data decisions with your team.

Overview What you will learn Requirements Learning format Enrollment

Make difficult data choices explainable

Responsible decisions rarely come from a checklist alone. This course gives cross-functional teams a shared way to identify affected people, test assumptions, document uncertainty, and choose proportionate safeguards.

You will work through realistic scenarios in product analytics, automated decisions, experimentation, and data sharing. Each scenario ends with a decision record you can adapt to your own work.

What you will be able to do

Frame the decision

Name the purpose, affected groups, alternatives, and evidence gaps before choosing a path.

Test proportionality

Compare expected benefit with privacy, fairness, autonomy, and operational risk.

Design safeguards

Choose controls that are measurable, owned, and appropriate to the actual level of risk.

Explain the outcome

Write a concise decision record that a reviewer and affected person can understand.

Who this course is for

  • Product, data, policy, research, or operations practitioners who influence how data is used.
  • No legal or technical specialization is required; examples explain necessary terms in context.
  • Bring one current or recent data decision so the final exercise connects to real work.

A paced, practice-led format

Each week combines a concise lesson, one facilitated case discussion, and a workplace exercise. Facilitators respond to submitted decision records within two working days. All live discussions are recorded, with an equivalent written path for people who cannot attend.

Completion requires four scenario decisions and one final decision record. You may pause between cohorts and resume from your last completed exercise.
